01 / Overview

Project overview

Sola Games is an e-commerce website dedicated to selling video games.

When we built Sola Games, it was important for us to emphasize three key aspects: ease of navigation, more attractive prices than other sites, and special features designed to give users a better shopping experience.

To make browsing even smoother, we developed an intuitive “sort by” system and advanced filters, allowing customers to quickly find exactly what they’re looking for, whether it’s the newest releases, bestsellers, or hidden gems.

Main goals

  1. 01

    Ease of Navigation

    Create a simple, user-friendly shopping experience.

  2. 02

    Transparent Deals

    Our deals are always clear, simple, and transparent.

  3. 03

    Smart Shopping Tools

    Provide “sort by” options and advanced filters for quick and easy browsing.

  4. 04

    Enhanced User Experience

    Add special features that make game shopping enjoyable and seamless.

02 / Usability

Usability features

01

Effectiveness

Navigation on Sola Games is intuitive with an advanced “Sort by” system and smart filters. The sign-up, cart, and checkout process is clear and straightforward.

02

Learnability

New users can easily understand how to perform key actions such as searching for a game, creating an account, or making a purchase. Thanks to the site’s simple navigation structure and clean UI.

03

Flexibility

The site suits both beginners and advanced users, through easy browsing or advanced filters, making it accessible for everyone.

04

Memorability

Returning users can easily recall filters, cart, and checkout, ensuring smooth use without “re-learning.”

05

Error Handling & Confidence

Clear error messages guide users when something goes wrong (e.g., wrong password or missing field), keeping the process smooth and frustration-free.

03 / Pain points

Pain points & solution

pain_points.txt

Pain Points

  • Unclear categories and limited search make games hard to find.
  • Prices vary and lack transparency on discounts.
  • Too many steps and confusing registration.
  • No tailored recommendations for users.
  • Missing reviews and reliable ratings.
  • Excessive banners disrupt the shopping experience.
solution.txt

Solution

  • Smart filters and “Sort by” make finding games effortless.
  • Competitive prices with clear, transparent deals.
  • Streamlined sign-up, cart, and payment.
  • Tailored recommendations beyond basic search.
  • Trust and transparency, user reviews and ratings build confidence.
  • Clean, clutter-free layout for a smooth experience.

05 / Research

Survey

In a survey we conducted, 40 people participated. Below are the results of the survey.

User survey conclusions

Most gamers use Steam or Epic, prioritize price, reviews, and ease of use, and prefer PayPal or credit cards. Pain points include unclear bundles and limited payment options, while users want advanced filtering, clear information, and personalized recommendations.

06 / Persona

Persona

Target audience

Our main users are gamers aged 18–35, mostly students and professionals in tech, design, and creative fields (e.g., software engineers, game developers, graphic designers, IT specialists).

They are tech-savvy and passionate about gaming, and they expect a smooth, reliable, and transparent shopping experience.
