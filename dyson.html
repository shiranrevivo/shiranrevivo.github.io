01 / Overview

Branding process

Our student project centers on rebranding Dyson’s Supersonic HD07 hair dryer for women who care deeply about personal grooming and advanced technology. We’re crafting a sleek new identity, from a refined logo to dynamic visual assets that highlight HD07’s precision temperature control, aerodynamic form, and quiet performance.

By unifying typography, color palette, and imagery into a cohesive system, we aim to reinforce Dyson’s legacy of innovation and luxury, appealing directly to style-conscious women who demand premium, high-tech hair-care solutions.
