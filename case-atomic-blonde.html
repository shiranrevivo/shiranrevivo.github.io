01 / Overview

Project overview

As part of our UX/UI studies, we were assigned the task of creating a one-pager website for a movie released between 2010 and 2020. We chose Atomic Blonde, a visually powerful and stylistic action film that surprisingly has no official website. The goal was to bring the film into the digital space in a way that feels immersive, cinematic, and true to its mood.

The process began with research into the film’s storyline, characters, creative team, and visual world. This helped us understand the tone and target audience, and informed every design decision I made. We then developed high-fidelity wireframes, focusing on clear user flow, usability, and storytelling without revealing spoilers.

The final visual design was inspired by the film’s neon-noir aesthetic. We used high-contrast color palettes, sharp typography, and bold layouts to evoke the intensity and energy of Atomic Blonde, while making sure the page was intuitive and engaging. The result is a one-pager that not only promotes the film but delivers a full digital experience.

02 / Usability

Usability features

Effectiveness

Every section flows seamlessly, guiding users through the experience without confusion. Whether booking a screening, browsing merch, or exploring cast details. Each action feels natural and direct, reducing cognitive load and keeping the focus on the film.

Ease of Learning

New users can grasp the interface within moments. Core actions like ticket purchase or streaming access are clearly marked, ensuring users don’t waste time figuring out what to do. The quicker the user understands, the faster they engage.

Flexibility

Whether you’re a die-hard fan or a first-time viewer, the interface adapts to your needs. From casual scroll-throughs to in-depth exploration, the experience works for beginners and power users alike.

Memorability

The bold neon aesthetic, high-contrast visuals, and sharp typography make the site unforgettable. Users will not only remember how the page looked, but how it made them feel.

Satisfaction

The immersive design, interactive elements (trailer, gallery), and exclusive event experience provide high user satisfaction and emotional engagement.

03 / Pain points

Pain points

Lack of Accessibility

The film is no longer available in theaters or online platforms, making it completely inaccessible to new audiences who want to watch it.

Declining Revenue

The film has stopped generating revenue, creating a need to find new ways to monetize its value and reach.

Loss of Visibility

As time passed, the film faded from public attention. Now requiring renewed marketing efforts to spark interest and drive engagement.

04 / Research

UX research

the_research.txt

The research

In this research, we chose to focus on people who have already watched Atomic Blonde to gain insights into our target audience’s opinions about the plot, characters, and overall viewing experience. We collected data from audience reviews on IMDb, social media comments, and fan forums to better understand expectations, reactions, and what makes the film resonate.

results.txt

Results

Our research showed that Atomic Blonde captivated audiences with its high-octane action, bold visual style, and Charlize Theron’s commanding lead performance. Fans were especially drawn to the film’s 1980s Berlin setting, pulse-pounding soundtrack, and gritty, cinematic fight sequences.

Audiences consistently praised Lorraine Broughton as a refreshingly fierce and complex female protagonist, cementing the film’s place as a standout in the spy thriller genre. Atomic Blonde continues to attract a dedicated fanbase, particularly among viewers who love stylish storytelling, graphic novel adaptations, and female-led espionage narratives.
